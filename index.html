Privacy Policy

DXF CALC ("we", "our", "the App") is a professional CAD/surveying calculation tool. We take your privacy seriously.

1. Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically:

  • No analytics or tracking SDKs are embedded.
  • No advertising SDKs are embedded.
  • No account registration or profile data is collected.
  • No location data is collected (the App Store version does not include GPS features).

2. Local Data

All CAD files, drawings, and calculation data you open or create are stored locally on your device only. Nothing is uploaded to any server. If you delete the App, this data is removed with it.

3. In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases (subscription or one-time purchase) to unlock professional features. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store any payment information. For details, see Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Subscriptions & Auto-Renewal

  • Subscriptions renew automatically unless auto-renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase.
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
  • Any unused portion of a free trial period is forfeited when you purchase a subscription.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for professional use and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

Support

How do I unlock Pro features?
Use the in-app purchase options (Monthly, Yearly, or Lifetime). After purchase, features unlock immediately.

I purchased but features are still locked?
Open the purchase screen and tap "Restore Purchases".

Which file formats are supported?
DXF files are fully supported for viewing, measuring, and calculation workflows.

Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes — go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your iPhone and cancel anytime.
